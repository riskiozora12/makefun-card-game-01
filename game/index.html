LANTERN DUEL

Four suits, one deck of thirty cards, and a herald of ash to put out.

Play cards with lanterns. Creatures fight on the board; spells strike at once. Play two cards of the same suit in a turn to trigger a Surge and draw a card. Reduce Vex to 0 life before your own light goes out.

Click a card in your hand to play it · Click your creature, then an enemy, to attack · Guard creatures (Thorn) must be attacked first

An original game · Ember · Tide · Thorn · Gale

How to play

  • Goal: reduce Vex, the Ash Herald, from 30 life to 0. If your life hits 0, you lose.
  • Lanterns: each turn you gain one more lantern (up to 10) and they refill. Every card costs lanterns — the blue number on its corner.
  • Creatures: click a creature card to put it on your board. It can attack from your next turn (unless it has Swift). Click your creature, then click an enemy creature or Vex himself to attack. Creatures fight back when attacked.
  • Guard: Thorn creatures with a shield must be attacked before anything else.
  • Spells: click a spell, then click its target. Red spells hurt, blue spells heal or draw, yellow spells trick the board.
  • Surge: play two cards of the same suit in one turn and you draw a card.
  • Fatigue: when your deck runs out, drawing hurts you — 1, then 2, then 3…
  • End turn: press END TURN when you are done. Vex plays, then it is your turn again.

VICTORY

The lanterns are relit. Vex is waiting.
